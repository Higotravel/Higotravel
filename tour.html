Is the $250 per person?

No, and that is the whole point of Buy 1 Take 1. The $250 Christmas Promo covers two people for the full 3 days and 2 nights package, so it works out to $125 each. The deal is always sold as 2 pax. The regular price for the same stay is $280 for two.

We are a group of 3. Which deal fits?

The family deal is priced for 4 ($450, with 2 travelling free). If you are 3, use the booking steps anyway: family deals are available for other group sizes too, and we will price it for you.

What exactly is included?

The main package includes the hotel stay for 2 nights, the Ba Na Hills Tour with the Golden Bridge, and the Hoi An Tour. Message HiGo Travel on WhatsApp for the full rundown for your dates before you book.

Yes. The Hue tour is available as an optional add-on to the main Ba Na Hills and Hoi An package. Switch it on in the booking steps, or ask on WhatsApp. Hue has no fixed price on this page: HiGo confirms the add-on price with you on WhatsApp before you pay anything.

Can we change the itinerary?

Why are there two prices for 3 days and 2 nights, $250 and $280?

They are two prices for the same stay, not a mistake. $250 for 2 people is the Christmas Promo price for Buy 1 Take 1. $280 for 2 people is the Regular price for the same 3 days and 2 nights, and it is the base the longer stays are built on. Pick whichever applies when you book, and the label (Christmas Promo or Regular) goes into your WhatsApp message so there is no mix-up.

Yes.
